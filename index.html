Atlas Personal AI Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Atlas is a personal assistant that runs on your iPhone. There is no Atlas account and no Atlas server: nothing you say to Atlas, and nothing it keeps, is ever received by the developer.

What Atlas keeps, and where

Your reminders, notes, the facts you tell it, your conversations and its copy of your calendar are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your phone, and only when

  • Online AI services — only after you say yes. If your phone has no language model of its own, Atlas asks before sending any question to free online AI services (Kilo, Pollinations, OVHcloud). Once you agree, the text of what you ask is sent to them so they can answer. Nothing else is sent — no contacts, no files, no location. Say “stop using online models” to turn this off at any time.
  • Web search — when you ask Atlas to look something up. Your search words go to DuckDuckGo, and Atlas reads the pages it finds. Web search can be turned off in Settings.
  • Weather — when you ask for it. The town you set goes to Open-Meteo. If you haven't set one, Atlas asks ipapi.co roughly where your internet connection is, over an encrypted connection.
  • The phone's own model — only if you ask for it. Saying “get your own model” downloads a model file from Hugging Face, on Wi-Fi only. Nothing about you is sent.

Kept on the phone, never sent

  • Speech recognition runs on the device; your voice is not sent anywhere.
  • Apple's on-device model, where your iPhone has Apple Intelligence, runs on the phone.
  • Calendar access is asked for only when you tap “Bring in your calendar”, and is used only to keep Atlas's calendar and the phone's in step, on this phone.
  • The microphone and camera are used only while you're talking to Atlas or taking a photo to give it.

No tracking

Atlas has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. It does not sell or share your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
